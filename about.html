ABOUT THE MUSEUM

Founded in 2017 by passionate enthusiasts of the arts, the Galerie d'Art is an all-around art gallery praising and appreciating any and all artforms, be it striking paintings all the way to the grand ensembles of theater. The Galerie d'Art is a space where we truly believe that art everything comes from the heart!
